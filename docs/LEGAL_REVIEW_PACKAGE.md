## Questions for counsel

**Privacy and data**

1. Which state privacy laws apply to a US launch of a health-adjacent app, and what notices, rights (access, correction, deletion) and sensitive-data consents are required?
2. Is the data the app holds (weight, food, wellness answers, photos) regulated health data under any US law or state consumer-health-data law? What consent wording is required?
3. Is a 30-day account-deletion waiting period acceptable, how must it be disclosed, and what may be retained (moderation records, audit log, backups) and for how long?
4. Retention periods for Community moderation records and the tamper-evident audit log.

**Minors and child safety** 5. Is the 18+ declared-birth-date approach sufficient, and what must happen when a user is found to be under 13 or under 18? 6. Reporting duties for apparent child sexual abuse material (for example to the NCMEC CyberTipline), preservation requirements, and how they interact with the runbook's "do not copy or forward" rule. 7. State laws on minors' online safety or age verification that could apply to an 18+ service.

**Community and user content** 8. Terms: intermediary liability, takedown process, whether a designated agent (DMCA) registration is needed for user-posted photos, governing law and any arbitration clause. 9. The moderation approach (automatic filtering, human review of every photo, 4 h / 48 h internal targets that are not promises): any duty-of-care or consumer-protection concerns, and whether the targets may be mentioned publicly. 10. Appeals through a support mailbox: any procedural requirements.

**Health and nutrition claims** 11. Wording of calorie, nutrition and fitness guidance ("provisional, not reviewed by a clinician, not medical advice") against consumer-protection and health-claim rules, including advertising and the store listing. 12. Whether the eating-concern safeguards and support information raise any additional obligations.

**Media and people** (when the content library is made) 13. Instructor agreements (name, image, voice, likeness), choreography ownership, performer and location releases, music licences (composition and master, sync, in-app, worldwide, term, territory), and takedown.

**Apple and distribution** 14. Review of the App Review notes and the App Privacy answers for accuracy.

## What to send counsel

This package, the listed documents, and a short plain-language description of what the app does and does not do (the review notes are a good start). State clearly which features are off (comments, video, public Community) and that nothing is live.

## 2. Would a second Supabase account be permitted, and would it get a free slot?

- **Free allowance:** Supabase's billing documentation says each account gets two free projects, counted across **all organizations where you are an owner or administrator**, and paused projects do not count (documentation as summarized in search results). That matches the refusal I received ("2 project limit" for `ebos4mum@gmail.com`). A different person-identity account would have its own allowance. A new _organization_ under the same account would not help.
- **Terms:** I could not retrieve the Terms of Service. Supabase's Acceptable Use Policy (version dated 2026-06-01, per search results) prohibits creating accounts by automated means, registering them in bulk, or "maintaining an excessive number of accounts as a single user" without Supabase's permission, and prohibits disposable email addresses. **One extra account used for staging is not obviously "excessive", but deliberately opening an account to get around the free-project limit is the kind of thing that clause is aimed at, and it is not confirmed either way.** Use a real, permanent address, not a throwaway one.
- **Conclusion:** probably workable, but not confirmed. Before asking you to create one, the safe step is a short written question to Supabase support ("may I use a second account for a staging environment of the same product?"). Support is free and gives you a record. Until you have an answer, I would not recommend it. Risk if wrong: suspension of the second account, which would lose only staging data.

## 4. Recommendation

1. **Do not repurpose `bxhnrj…` on assumption.** If, after your two-minute check, you can say it is unused and disposable, **Option A is the lowest-cost, fastest and least terms-risky route**, and I would then use it only after your explicit approval, after adding it to the staging guard as a deliberate, named exception (today the guard refuses it by design).
2. **If it is not disposable or you cannot confirm, use Option B only after Supabase support confirms in writing that a second account is acceptable.** Until then, nothing is created.
3. **Treat Option C as a launch-time decision**, not a staging shortcut: when you decide where production lives, decide the plan at the same time and put staging under it.
4. **In parallel, at no cost and with no account:** much of the real-backend checking (real Auth including MFA, real database access rules, real Storage) can be done on your own machine with Supabase's local stack, which runs in Docker. It cannot reproduce the hosted Edge runtime's memory and time limits or platform behaviour, so it complements staging and does not replace it. Docker is installed in this environment but I have not started or pulled anything; it would download several images, so I would ask first.

# Minors policy for Community (DRAFT for legal review)

Status: draft. Not legal advice.

## Rule

The app is for **adults aged 18 and over**. The app already refuses birth dates that make someone under 18. Community adds no way around that: there is no separate teen or family mode.

## What this means in practice

1. **No under-18s as members.** If anything shows a member is under 18 (their own post, a report, a moderator's observation), the account is closed through the normal account-closure process and their Community content is removed.
2. **No children in photos or videos.** Moderators remove any photo or video in which a child appears, even if an adult posted it and even if the child's face is not visible. Dance videos are the main risk: reviewers must check backgrounds and groups.
3. **No contact with minors.** Community has no direct messages, follows or hashtags, so members cannot privately contact each other. Comments are public and moderated.
4. **Reports about a possible child.** A report or flag that suggests a child is involved is **urgent**. The moderator hides the content immediately, does not contact anyone outside the app, records the action, and tells the escalation contact the same day. Any legal duty to report (which differs by country) is for the escalation contact and counsel to decide; the moderator does not decide alone.
5. **No collection of ages.** Community does not ask for or show anyone's age.

## Open items for the reviewer

1. Confirm 18+ is the right threshold in every launch country, and what age assurance is expected beyond a declared birth date.
2. Mandatory-reporting duties and the correct authority to report to, per country.
3. How long to keep records of removed content that involved a possible child.
4. Whether the in-app report reasons need a specific "child safety" reason (currently covered by "Someone may need help" and "Shares private information").

# Open Food Facts: activation checklist (NOT activated)

Decision (owner, 2026-11-15): Open Food Facts is the preferred first packaged-food database. **It stays OFF** (`EXPO_PUBLIC_BARCODE_PROVIDER` defaults to `local` in development and `off` in production) until every line below is verified from the live documentation and the owner approves activation.

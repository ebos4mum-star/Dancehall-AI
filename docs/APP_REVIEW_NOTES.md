## What the app is

DanceHall Fit AI is an adult (18+) fitness and nutrition app built around dance workouts, with food logging, daily goals, habits and a rule-based coach. It is not a medical app and makes no diagnostic or treatment claims. Calorie and nutrition guidance is labelled provisional and general, with prompts to see a doctor or dietitian.

## Using the app without an account

Most of the app works as a guest with data kept on the device. Community can be read without an account.

## Community (user-generated content)

Community is one chronological feed where members share short text posts, milestones from their workouts and progress photos. There are no comments, no direct messages, no follows, no hashtags, no rankings and no video.

- **Filtering:** text is screened automatically and unsafe text (self-harm, eating-disorder content, links, contact details) is refused; weight-loss claims and health numbers are held for review. **Every photo is reviewed by a person before anyone else can see it.**
- **Reporting:** every post has Report, with reasons including "someone may need help" and "a child may be involved". Urgent reports hide the post immediately and alert the moderators.
- **Blocking:** any member can be blocked from the post menu; blocking is mutual and silent. Members can delete their own posts and leave Community at any time.
- **Moderation:** a primary and a backup moderator review the queue with a second factor required. Internal review targets are 4 hours for urgent reports and 48 hours otherwise. Objectionable content and abusive users are removed; accounts can be muted or suspended.
- **Contact:** [support email], shown inside Community and at [support URL].
- **Account deletion:** Me, then Privacy and settings, then **Delete my account**. Deletion starts a [30]-day cancellable waiting period, after which the account and all data, including Community content, are permanently deleted.
- Community members are adults (18+). Photos are resized and stripped of location data on the device and again on our server.

# Privacy disclosures for Community (DRAFT for legal review)

Status: draft wording to add to the privacy notice when Community launches. Not legal advice. A qualified reviewer must check it against the requirements of every country where Community will be offered (data-protection, online-safety, and children's-privacy rules) before it is used.

## What Community never uses

- Your health information. Weight, food logs, wellness answers, readiness answers, goals and calorie targets are **never** shared by Community, never attached to a post and never shown to moderators. "Share My Achievement" copies only the title and style of a workout, and the minutes only if you switch that on and confirm.
- Your real name, email address or any internal account identifier. Your account has no email address; Community does not add one.
- Photo metadata. Location and camera details are removed from photos on your phone and again on our server. Videos that contain a saved location are refused.

## Choices you have

- Nothing is posted automatically. Every post shows a preview and needs you to confirm that everyone can see it.
- You can browse without an account.
- You can delete a post, delete a comment, leave Community, or delete your account.
- Photos and videos are optional.

## Who processes the data

Community runs on the same hosting and database as the rest of the app. It uses no advertising, analytics or AI service. (If a photo-scanning or video-processing provider is added later, this section must name it before launch.)

Initial market: United States. Counsel should check state privacy laws (including those with specific rights to access and delete), children's privacy rules, and the retention of moderation and legal-hold records.

# Community Guidelines (DRAFT for owner and legal review)

Status: draft. Not yet shown to members as final. The short version already in the app (`community.guidelines` in `apps/mobile/src/i18n/en.ts`) must match this once approved.

## The short version (shown in the app)

Be kind and encouraging. Share your own progress, not numbers about your body. Leave out weight, calories, links, contact details and anything private. No advice about fast weight loss, no medical claims, no comparing bodies. A person checks photos and videos before they appear. You can delete your posts any time, and you can report or block anyone.

## The full version

**Who this is for.** DanceHall Fit AI is for adults (18+). Community is a friendly place to celebrate moving more. It is not a place for medical advice, weight-loss challenges or competition.

**What to share.** Finished a class? Learned a step? Feeling proud of showing up? Share it. Photos and short dance videos are welcome when you are happy for everyone to see them.

**What to leave out.**

- Numbers about your body: weight, measurements, calories, BMI, "I lost 10 kg".
- Advice about food, supplements, medicines, fasting, cleanses or fast results.
- Links, email addresses, phone numbers, social handles, "message me" or anything selling something.
- Other people's private information, or photos of people who have not agreed to be shown.
- Anything about hurting yourself or others, or about starving, purging or "thinspiration".
- Comparing bodies, mocking bodies, or commenting on someone's size.
- Pretending to be staff, the app, or another member.

**If someone may need help.** If you are struggling, you do not have to post. The app shows support information when it notices words that suggest you might need it, and your goals work without Community. If you are worried about someone else's post, use Report. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

**Your name.** You choose a public username, not your real name. You can change it once every 30 days. Names that copy staff, the app, or another member are not allowed.

**Reviews and removals.** Every photo and video is checked by a person before it appears. Posts that mention weight loss, health numbers or health claims are also checked first. Reports hide a post for review. We may remove content that breaks these guidelines, and mute or suspend accounts that keep doing so. We explain what to change when we can.

**Your control.** Delete your posts any time. Block anyone: you will not see each other, and they are not told. Leave Community at any time: your username, posts, comments, photos and videos are removed, and your account stays.

**Reports are private.** The member you report is never told who reported them.

**Appeals.** If you think a decision was wrong, use the contact route in the app's Help. A different person than the original moderator should look at it when there is more than one moderator. (Open decision: see the moderation runbook.)

5. **Moderation.** We may review, hold, hide, remove or refuse content, and mute or suspend accounts, to keep Community safe. We are not obliged to publish anything. We explain our reason when we can.
6. **Reports and blocks.** You may report content and block members. We keep reporters private.
7. **Usernames.** Usernames are public. We may refuse or change a name that is misleading, offensive or impersonates someone. You may change yours once every 30 days.
8. **Leaving.** You may leave Community or delete your account at any time. Your content is then removed as described in the privacy notice.
9. **No guarantees.** Community may be paused, limited to text, or closed at any time, for example while moderation catches up. We do not promise that every post will be reviewed within a set time.
10. **Illegal content.** We may remove content and keep records of it where the law requires, and may share information with authorities where the law requires.
